## Sound Effects Needed

### 1. Start Set / Start Workout

**Reference**: Sports whistle
**Purpose**: Immediate attention, clean “go” signal

**Requirements**

* Duration: **0.5–1.0 seconds**
* Sharp attack, no tail
* Should cut through ambient noise
* Not shrill or painful at volume

---

### 2. Rep Pulse (Standard)

**Reference**: Subtle click / beep / short tone
**Purpose**: Rhythm cue for each repetition

**Requirements**

* Duration: **50–150 ms**
* Neutral, unobtrusive
* Must not become fatiguing over many reps
* Clear transient, minimal harmonic complexity

---

### 3. Rep Pulse (Last Rep – Urgent)

**Reference**: Faster, brighter version of standard pulse
**Purpose**: Signals final rep without user looking at screen

**Requirements**

* Same base sound family as standard rep pulse
* Slightly higher pitch and/or louder
* Clearly distinguishable from normal pulse
* Duration: **≤200 ms**

---

### 4. End of Set

**Reference**: Boxing bell ring
**Purpose**: Clear closure of a set, momentary relief

**Requirements**

* Duration: **1.0–2.5 seconds**
* Strong initial strike
* Short decay (no long reverb tails)
* Should feel definitive, not celebratory

---

### 5. End of Workout

**Reference**: Victory / achievement fanfare
**Purpose**: Emotional reward for completing workout

**Requirements**

* Duration: **2–4 seconds**
* Trumpet or brass-based fanfare preferred
* Major key, triumphant but not cheesy
* Clean ending (no fade-out)

---

## Technical Requirements

* Format: **WAV**
* Sample Rate: **48 kHz**
* Bit Depth: **16-bit or 24-bit**
* Loudness: Normalized for mobile playback
* Deliver **dry mixes** (minimal or no reverb)

Optional but preferred:

* Slightly quieter alternative mix for each sound
* Stems or project files (if applicable)

---

## Style & Constraints

* Designed for **mobile phone speakers**
* Must remain intelligible outdoors and during movement
* No copyrighted melodies or recognizable audio samples
* No novelty or cartoon effects

Think **fitness / game UI**, not film or EDM.
